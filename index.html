Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Snapzzle. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Snapzzle mobile application. Snapzzle is designed with privacy in mind as an offline-first puzzle game, ensuring your personal photos and game data remain on your device.

Information We Collect

Snapzzle requires certain device permissions to function, but it processes this data entirely locally on your device:

  • Camera and Photo Library Access: We request access to your device's camera and photo library so you can create custom sliding puzzles. None of your photos or images are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any external servers. All image processing and slicing happens directly on your device.
  • Game Data: Statistics such as total games played, total wins, and best solve times are saved locally on your device storage.

How We Use Information

The information accessed by Snapzzle is used strictly to provide the core gameplay experience (generating sliding puzzle challenges from your images) and maintaining your progress. We do not use your personal data or photos for any other purpose.

Advertising

Snapzzle is a free application. To support the ongoing development of the app, we use a third-party advertising service. Advertisements may be displayed in the form of banners, interstitial (full-screen) ads, and rewarded video ads.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within Snapzzle. Google AdMob may collect and use certain data (such as your IP address, device identifiers, and advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, track ad performance, and prevent fraud.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Third-Party Services

Aside from Google AdMob for serving advertisements, Snapzzle does not integrate with any third-party analytics, background tracking, or account management services.

Data Sharing

Because Snapzzle operates primarily offline (with the exception of serving ads), we do not collect or share your personal information, photos, or gameplay data with any third parties.

Data Storage and Retention

All your user-generated content (photos) and gameplay statistics are stored locally on your device. Snapzzle does not retain any of this data on external servers. The data is retained for as long as the application is installed on your device.

Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data by keeping it local to your device. Since your personal photos and game statistics are never transmitted over the internet by our application, they are not exposed to network interception by us.

Children's Privacy

Snapzzle does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. Note that advertisements served by Google AdMob are subject to Google's policies regarding children's privacy and age-appropriate ads.

User Rights / Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. Because all game statistics and photo access happen locally, you can easily delete all data associated with Snapzzle at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app entirely from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
